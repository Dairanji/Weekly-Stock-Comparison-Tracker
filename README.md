# Weekly-Stock-Comparison-Tracker (Dashboard creation using MS Excel)
## Project Objective
The Weekly-Stock-Comparison-Tracker wants to track and compare Total Stocks & Aging Stocks weekly to get a clear understanding & follow up accordingly. So that, each any every dealer in West Bengal that are associated with RCF will get an update & The organization will get a clear visualisation of Stock clearance.


## Dataset Used
- <a href="https://github.com/Dairanji/Weekly-Stock-Comparison-Tracker/blob/main/WEEKLY%20180%2B%20AGING%20STOCK%20TRACKER%20DEALER%20WISE.xlsx">Dataset</a>

## Questions (KPIs)
- Which dealers are holding stock for more than 180 days — and exactly how much?
- Which districts have the highest aging stock — where should the team focus first?
- How much of the aging stock is critical — what has crossed 366 days and needs urgent action?
- Which products are aging the most?
- Is the situation improving or getting worse — did a dealer's aging stock go up or down compared to last week?
- How much total stock does a dealer hold vs. how much is aged — what percentage of their inventory is a problem?
- Who do we contact and how — which dealer needs a follow-up email right now?
- Where is the stock stuck — at the warehouse level or at the retail point?

## Process
- Verify data for any missing values and anomalies, and sort out the same.
- Made sure data is consistent and clean with respect to data type, data format and values used.
- Created formulas for each cells to determine total stocks of each type of fertilizer for both previous & current week.
- Created pivot tables according to the questions asked.
- Merge all pivot tables into one dashboard and make it dynamic.

## Project Insight
- 📍 Covers all of West Bengal — tracks ~95 dealers spread across 17 districts in a single unified file, eliminating the need for separate district-level reports
- ⏳ 180-day threshold as the trigger — any stock crossing 180 days is automatically flagged, giving the team a clear and consistent rule to act on
- 🔴 Two-level urgency system — stock is split into 181–365 days and 366+ days buckets, so the team always knows what to prioritize first
- 🌾 Multi-product visibility — UREA, S-15, 10-26-26, MOP, and DAP are all tracked together, giving a complete picture of a dealer's aging inventory in one row
- 📧 Email outreach built right in — the one-click SEND EMAIL button removes friction from follow-ups, making dealer communication faster and more consistent
- 📈 Week-on-week comparison — previous and current week data sit side by side, making it easy to see if a dealer's aging stock is growing, shrinking, or stuck
- 🏭 Warehouse + Retail split — separate WH+RT sheets distinguish between stock held at the warehouse level vs. at retail, helping identify where the bottleneck actually is


## Final Conclusion
Managing aging stock across 95+ dealers over 17 districts used to mean chasing data from multiple sources. This tracker puts everything in one place — updated weekly, easy to read, and built to drive action. It's a simple tool that solves a real operational problem.
